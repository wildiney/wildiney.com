Pick an AI agent at your company, in pilot or already in operation, and ask who owns it and how much it costs per month. In Zappts' survey, 61.8% of Brazilian companies have agents in those two phases: 13.8% have scaled in operations and 48% are somewhere between pilot and scale.

The Panorama da IA no Brasil surveyed 167 executives, recruited through the channels of Zappts itself, a consultancy that sells AI agent projects. The share of companies that don't measure AI ROI nearly doubled in a year, from 30.6% to 55.1%. In the same survey, 26.2% have no defined budget for agents, and 55.2% have no AI gateway. Pablo Augusto, Zappts' CEO, says AI agents "are not just isolated IT projects, they become part of a new digital workforce."

A workforce has a manager, and before the pilot becomes production that manager must answer for the agent, with a defined budget and an ROI target to hit. Without that, nobody can say whether this workforce pays for itself.
